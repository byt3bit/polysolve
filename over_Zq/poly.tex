\begin{abstract}
In this work we present an algorithm for solving the Learning With Errors (LWE) problem and its variants RingLWE (RLWE) and ModuleLWE (MLWE) over the ring of integers modulo a composite integer \(q\), denoted \(\Z_q\). Previous algebraic analyses have focused on LWE over prime fields, but our approach extends these techniques to composite moduli. Moreover, our algorithm completely avoids polynomial divisions by exploting a sound pair-selection strategy, which allows us to perform $S$-polynomial computations and reductions by using linear algebra. We also provide a detailed complexity analysis of our algorithm and compare it with existing approaches for solving LWE over finite fields. Finally, we explore how to map the LWE problem in \(\Z_q\) to a combination of distinct finite fields \(\F_{p^e}\) using the Chinese Remainder Theorem, and analyze the practical considerations of this approach.
\end{abstract}
